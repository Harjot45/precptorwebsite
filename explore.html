What to look for
before you say yes.

Clinical fit is more than specialty and distance. Use these questions when evaluating an actual opportunity.

01

Credential alignment

Will your school accept the proposed mentor’s licensure, specialization and experience?

02

Learning environment

Does the clinic or hospital provide relevant patients, experiences and supervision?

03

Schedule compatibility

Can the available days and hours realistically support your program’s clinical requirements?

04

Approval readiness

Who completes the paperwork, and how will your school formally approve the placement?
