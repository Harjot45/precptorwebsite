# Research and design direction

Reviewed 29 October 2026. Public website content can change. These references inform interaction patterns, not claims about Placement Preceptor.

## Main interaction references

### NPHub
https://www.nphub.com/find-preceptors

Its search-and-book guide describes location and specialty search, optional start-date filtering, clinical detail review, and a request containing hours, dates, and school requirements. Adaptation: a filterable sample explorer, detailed rotation dialogs, and a structured placement brief.

### Clinical Match Me
https://www.clinicalmatchme.com/
https://www.clinicalmatchme.com/become-a-preceptor/

Its public site emphasizes request-led matching, student choice among offers, and a separate preceptor journey. Adaptation: saved options, side-by-side comparison, and distinct student/preceptor flows. No live offers, competing-provider model, guaranteed approval, fees, or compensation are claimed in this prototype.

### MatchNP
https://www.matchnp.com/how-it-works/

Its workflow distinguishes students, preceptors, and universities and explains profile creation, matching, and coordination. Adaptation: audience-specific process panels and a guided sequence that makes requirements and approvals visible.

## Original design decisions

- Navy, warm orange, soft blue, and neutral surfaces create a distinct identity.
- A layered photographic hero introduces clinical learning; editorial typography and subtle motion provide visual depth.
- The explorer provides useful interaction immediately, rather than relying only on animation.
- Sample listing artwork uses specialty symbols instead of fabricated clinician headshots or endorsements.
- The feature section explains requirements, coordination, and mentorship through different layouts.
- Process tabs change real content; resource cards open complete short guides.
- Motion respects reduced-motion preferences. Core information remains available without scroll animations.

## Business details deliberately left unresolved

Live network size, availability, pricing, turnaround time, school partnerships, guaranteed placement, refunds, and clinician compensation need confirmation. None is invented in the mockup. School approval remains a distinct requirement, not an automatic outcome of submitting a brief.
