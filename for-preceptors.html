Good questions from
future mentors.

Some expectations are determined by schools and clinical sites, so it helps to ask early.

What qualifications would I need to teach?+

Each school sets its own preceptor eligibility criteria, which may include active licensure, specialty alignment and professional experience. Confirm the specific requirements before committing.

How much time would a student need from me?+

That depends on the student's clinical hours, weekly attendance, learning needs and the supervision rules that apply. Agree on a feasible schedule before the placement is approved.

Will I be required to accept a student?+

No. This prototype lets you explore a proposed expression-of-interest workflow, but it creates no obligation and does not connect you to a real student.

Are compensation or legal terms established?+

No. Compensation, agreements, liability requirements and related business terms have not been defined for this concept and must be confirmed before a live launch.
